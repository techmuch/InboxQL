About this screen

Purpose. Value 2, nothing happens silently. Agents act through the CLI, out of sight. This is where everything they did is visible afterwards, grouped by the pass that did it, with its cost, and undoable — one change or the whole pass.

What “Undo” means

  • Changed mail (archive, label): reversed here and on the server, as iql mail undo does now.
  • Answers (a judgement, an extraction): undo removes the answer and makes the conversation pending again. Better is usually to correct it — open the conversation.
  • Proposed items have not happened; undo withdraws the proposal.
  • Sync from your other devices is shown for completeness and cannot be undone here.

Not a notification feed

Nothing here demands attention; it is a record. Things that need you are in Today and To review. The status line in the app bar gives a one-line summary of the last pass.
