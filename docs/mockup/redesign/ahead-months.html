About this screen

Purpose. The long view: what the next two months hold, where the busy and empty stretches are, and what is coming that needs preparing for. It is for planning, not for working through items — that is the two-week view and Today.

Callouts

  1. 1 The summary sentence is about shape: busy or quiet, away or home, and what has a lead time. On the last day of a month, “this month” is one line and the next two months are shown in full.
  2. 2 Each month is a grid for the eye, with a sentence above it that says the same thing in words. Important dates are tinted and starred; agent-found dates are violet. At phone width the grid shows only day numbers and stars, and the list below carries the words.
  3. 3 What matters is the month without routine: important dates, due dates, trips, holidays and anything agent-found. Weekly repeats (Ward council) are left out here; they are in the grid and the calendar.

Screen reader

The month grid is a real table with a caption and day-of-week column headers, inside a focusable region. It is optional: the sentence and the “What matters” list say everything the grid shows except the routine.
