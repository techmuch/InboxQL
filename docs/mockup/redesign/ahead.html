About this screen

Purpose. Today answers what needs me now. Ahead answers what is coming, and is any of it a problem. It puts the calendar, card due dates, replies with a deadline, trips and deliveries found in mail, and the dates you marked important on one page, in date order. It is reached from Today's “Ahead” section, the navigation, or g a.

Callouts

  1. 1 One sentence first, comparing the range with your usual: “busiest in a month”. It names the problems (an overlap) and the next important date, so a screen reader user has the whole picture before any list.
  2. 2 Countdowns are for dates you marked important, not for everything with a date. Five at most are shown here; the rest are under “All important dates”. The agent can suggest one from mail (dashed, violet); it is not counted down until you accept. A date can carry a lead time — “start renewing 6 weeks before” — and the card then counts down to both. A countdown turns amber only inside its lead time or the last two weeks. Important dates notify you as they approach — when a lead time starts, a week before, the day before and that morning, changeable per date. Birthdays and anniversaries come from contacts planned.
  3. 3 Next week at a glance: hours booked and what needs you, per day, so the busy and the empty days stand out before you read the detail. The bar is height only; the number is always printed beside it.
  4. 4 Every item says its kind in a word. Things the agent found or proposed are marked violet and are not counted as booked until you approve them.

“This week” on a Saturday

The week is calendar weeks (starting Monday, changeable). Days already past fold into one line, so late in the week “this week” is short and “next week” does the work. The same rule makes Two months show the rest of October in one line and then November and December in full.

What changed from today's app

  • Nothing like this exists today. The Calendar shows events only; due dates are on the Board; trips and deliveries are extracted but only visible by query.
  • “Important” is a new mark you put on an event, card, trip, message or a plain date planned. It is one flag, not a new kind of thing, so is:important works in any query.
  • Replies with a deadline (“by Monday”) need the waiting element to record the deadline it read planned.

Screen reader

Each countdown is one list item with one spoken name: “Q4 report to the board, Tue, Nov 10, in 10 days. Within its lead time.” Each day is a heading level 3 with the full date (“Monday, November 2”), so heading navigation moves a day at a time; the items under it are a list. The at-a-glance strip is a list of seven items with the hours as text; the bars are hidden from assistive technology.
