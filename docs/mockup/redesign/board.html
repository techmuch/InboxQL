About this screen

Purpose. Your own to-dos and projects. The board stays a place for planning, but cards also show up in their subjects (Kitchen remodel lists its cards) and in Ahead by due date, so you rarely need to come here to find one.

Callouts

  1. 1 The sentence counts what is open, due and proposed.
  2. 2 Proposed cards are the first list, dashed and violet. Accepting one is a decision you make; nothing the agent proposes becomes a to-do on its own.

What changed from today's app

  • Proposed cards are also counted in Review, so the queue is in one place.
  • Card labels are the same words as mail topics, so filtering by “Kitchen remodel” here and opening the Kitchen remodel subject agree.

Screen reader

Each list is a region with a heading and an ordered list of cards. Moving a card is never drag-only: m opens a “Move to” menu, and the result is announced (“Moved to Doing, position 1 of 3”).
