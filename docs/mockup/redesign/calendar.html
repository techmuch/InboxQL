                About this screen

                Purpose. The calendar stays a place, because time is a layout people read at a glance. It loses its own approval sidebar (proposals go to Review) and gains the evidence panel: every event says where it came from.

                Callouts

                1. 1 A sentence about the week first: hours, overlaps, proposals.
                2. 2 Proposals sit on the grid where they would go, marked, so you can see whether they fit before approving. They are not counted in hours booked.

                What changed from today's app

                • Proposals move from the Calendar sidebar to Review, and remain visible here.
                • Month and Agenda views are the two Ahead views; the calendar is the same data at day and week scale.

                Each day is a region with a heading (“Monday, November 2”) and its events as a list in time order, so the grid is read as an agenda. Each event’s name includes its time and, in words, whether it is proposed or overlaps.
