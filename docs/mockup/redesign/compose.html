About this screen

Purpose. Writing happens in the page, beside its context, not in a modal over it. A reply opened from a conversation keeps the conversation in the right-hand panel.

Callouts

  1. 1 The decision is a form control, not a sentence to edit. When an agent's draft needs something only you know, the choice is a labelled radio group taken from the words of the question. Choosing fills the draft. Send is off until it is answered.
  2. 2 Send waits 10 seconds with Undo in the status toast (and z), as now. “Send later” is planned.

Who wrote what

A draft records its origin (agent or you). Once you edit an agent draft it is yours; the outbox shows “started by the agent, edited by you” so an unedited agent draft is always recognisable.

Keyboard and screen reader

All fields are labelled; the decision is a fieldset with a legend, so a screen reader announces the question before the choices. ⌘↵ sends; Esc returns to the conversation and keeps the draft.
