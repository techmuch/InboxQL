About this screen

Purpose. All your mail, wherever it is: the place to look for something you archived, or to see where a message went. It is folder:allmail with the place and account as buttons.

Callouts

  1. 1 The sentence says what is left out and how much, with a way to include it — an empty search is never a mystery.
  2. 2 Where and account are toggle groups, not a folder tree. Mail has labels; a message can be in Sent and under Receipts at once.
  3. 3 Where is a column: every row says where the message is and, if something other than you moved it, who did.

What changed from today's app

  • The folder rows in the Desk rail become these buttons; labels stay under Subjects.
  • A table rather than a list, because looking things up is comparing columns.
