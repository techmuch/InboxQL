About these screens

  • Four places, not ten. Today, Review, Ask and Subjects. Everything else is reached from a subject or by searching.
  • Review is one item at a time on a phone, with large buttons (at least 44 px) and the decision first. Swiping is not required for anything; every swipe has a button.
  • Reading and triage, not configuration. Settings, annotators and the work-loop metrics are desktop screens; on a phone they show read-only.
  • Decided: the phone is a real target — InboxQL installed as an app from Chrome and reached over the person’s Tailscale network. How sign-in, the secure address and notifications work is still being designed.
