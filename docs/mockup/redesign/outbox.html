About this screen

Purpose. Everything outgoing that has not gone yet: what waits for your Send, what failed and why, and drafts. It is the same queue the CLI’s iql send fills, so an agent’s mail always stops here.

Callouts

  1. 1 The sentence names what needs you; “nothing is sending” is said, not implied by an empty list.
  2. 2 Agent mail waits for you. “Written by the agent · not edited” is shown until you change a word. Send asks once, showing the exact message, and then waits 10 seconds with Undo.

What changed from today's app

  • Replaces Intelligence → Waiting for approval and the Drafts folder in Desk.
  • A failure says the server’s reason and what to do, instead of only a status.
