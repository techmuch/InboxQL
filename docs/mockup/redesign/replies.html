About this screen

Purpose. Every conversation the waiting element judged to be on you, in one list — the full version of the first section of Today. A tab turns it round to what you are waiting for from others.

Callouts

  1. 1 One sentence of state for both directions, so the page never only shows half the picture.
  2. 2 Deadline first. A reply with a date in it (“by Monday”) sorts by that date; the rest by age. The deadline is read by the agent and shown with its quote in the evidence panel planned.
  3. 3 Old owed replies are asked about, not left to rot: a reply owed for two weeks is either still owed or it is not.
  4. 4 Bulk actions on a selection. “Not waiting on me” records your ruling on each conversation; nothing is sent.

What changed from today's app

  • Today this list exists only as a query (awaiting:me) and as counts on a contact card.
  • “Waiting on others” replaces looking through Sent for unanswered mail.
