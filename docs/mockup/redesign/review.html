About this screen

Purpose. Value 1, the person decides, made fast. Today these approvals live in four places — Intelligence → Waiting for approval, the Calendar sidebar, the Board's Proposed list, and Work loop checks. Here they are one queue, worked with the same keys.

Rules

  • Send needs its own confirmation. a on a message shows the exact recipients and gives the usual 10 seconds to undo; selecting many items and pressing a never sends mail — messages are excluded from bulk approval.
  • Checks are blind. The agent's answer is hidden until you give yours, so your ruling measures the agent rather than agreeing with it.
  • Agents leave decisions blank. A draft that needs something only you know — a choice, a date, a yes — carries a marked blank, and Send stays off until it is filled. The agent writes; it does not decide for you.
  • Who proposed it is always shown: an agent (by name), a rule (by name), or an extractor.
  • Rejecting is kept, as now: it is evidence the agent was wrong.

Screen reader

A multi-select listbox. Each option's name says the kind, the subject, and who proposed it; the detail panel on the right is a complementary landmark whose heading changes with the selection and is announced politely.
