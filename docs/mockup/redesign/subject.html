About this screen

Purpose. Objective 1, one subject at a time. A person, a topic, a label or any saved query opens as a page like this one. Mail, files, cards, events and money that today live in Desk, Files, Board, Calendar and the Dashboard appear here in one stream, filtered to this subject.

Callouts

  1. 1 Open loops first: what you owe and what you are waiting for, from the waiting element, each with the line that makes it a loop. The person's page is where you decide whether to reply, nudge or let it go.
  2. 2 One stream, filterable by kind. Filters are toggle buttons (aria-pressed); each adds a term to the query in the app bar, so the page is always the query you can see.

Leaving and coming back

The crumb says how you got here. Esc or u goes back to the exact place you left — the same row in Today, with focus on it. A subject is never a tab you must close.

Topic and query subjects

A topic (“Kitchen remodel”) uses the same page: the header lists the people in it instead of an address, and open loops cover every conversation tagged with the topic. A saved query subject shows its query as the header.
