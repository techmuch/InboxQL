About this screen

Purpose. A topic is a subject that is not a person: everything about one piece of your life or work, across people. It is the page for a project like a remodel, where the questions are what is next, what is it costing, and who is holding things up.

Callouts

  1. 1 Next is one sentence about what moves the project forward, built from open loops and dates — not from a summary of the mail.
  2. 2 Money is added up from receipts in the topic and compared with the quote the agent found. Each figure links to its message.

What changed from today's app

  • Today a topic is a tag on threads (topic:) with no page of its own.
  • Topics come from the agent’s topics element; you can add or remove any conversation, and your choice wins.
