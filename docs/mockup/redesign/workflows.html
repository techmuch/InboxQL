Workflows

Each workflow says what it is for, who starts it, the screens it passes through, and every step: what you do, the key, what you see, and what a screen reader hears. Spoken text is illustrative.

  1. Morning pass — Get to “nothing needs me” in one short session.
  2. Work one subject — Deal with everything about one person or topic, then return to exactly where you were.
  3. Reply with the agent’s draft — Send a reply the agent wrote, after deciding what only you can decide.
  4. Clear a group — Archive or label many messages at once, safely.
  5. Correct the agent — Fix a wrong conclusion so it stays fixed and teaches the agent.
  6. Check the agent — Measure how often the agent is right, honestly.
  7. Ask a question of the mail — Answer “how much / how many / who” without leaving the app.
  8. Find out what happened — Answer “why did this message disappear?”
  9. Look ahead — Once a week, see the next two weeks and the next two months, and settle anything that will be a problem.
  10. Ask in words — Get an answer to a question you cannot easily write as a query, with the evidence behind it.
  11. Put InboxQL on your phone — Use the mailbox from your phone, and get reminders there.
  12. Mark a date as important — Make a date count down on Today and Ahead, with a lead time if it needs preparing for.

Goal

Serves
Value 1 · Objective 7
Steps of Mark a date as important
#YouKeysYou seeScreen reader hears
1Mark iti on an event, card, trip or message; or “+ Mark a date as important”A small form: which date, repeats yearly, lead time (none, 1 week, 6 weeks, custom)“Mark as important, dialog. Date: Thursday January 14 2027. Lead time, combo box, none.”
2Set a lead timeChoose “6 weeks”The countdown shows both: the date and when to start“Start renewing by Thursday December 3, in 33 days.”
